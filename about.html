Company Overview

About Pines Energy Group LLC

East Texas Operations

Pines Energy Group LLC is an East Texas energy and logistics company focused on biodiesel production, used cooking oil collection, and regional fuel supply operations.

The company operates with a regional logistics model centered around Hideaway, Athens, Lindale, and surrounding East Texas communities.

Operational Focus

  • Used cooking oil collection
  • Regional biodiesel production
  • Fuel supply relationships with farms and ranches
  • Commercial logistics and processing operations
  • Long-term regional sourcing agreements
