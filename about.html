Who We Are

East Texas Energy, Operated Locally

Pines Energy Group LLC is a privately held energy company based in Hideaway, Texas. The company was founded to address two intersecting needs in the East Texas region: the disposal of commercial cooking oil and the demand for stable, affordable diesel fuel alternatives on working farms and ranches.

The business operates two distinct branches under one management structure. Pines Oil Recycling handles the collection and logistics side, working directly with restaurants to remove used cooking oil at no cost. Pines BioFuel manages the production and distribution of biodiesel fuel derived from that collected feedstock.

Every operation is conducted in-house. There are no third-party haulers, no outside processing contractors, and no brokered feedstock from outside the service region when local supply is available. This structure is intentional. It keeps quality accountable and costs predictable for both suppliers and customers.

Mission and Operations

A Clear Operational Purpose

Pines Energy Group was built around a straightforward model: collect a regional waste product, convert it into usable fuel, and deliver that fuel to customers who depend on it. No speculation. No brokerage. Operational infrastructure only.

01 — Supply

Regional Feedstock Collection

Used cooking oil is collected from restaurants and food service accounts across East Texas. Supply relationships are maintained directly, with company-owned storage containers placed at partner locations.

02 — Production

In-House Biodiesel Processing

Collected oil is processed at the Athens, TX facility through transesterification into finished biodiesel. Output meets ASTM D6751 standards for use in standard diesel-powered equipment without modification.

03 — Distribution

Direct Farm and Ranch Delivery

Finished fuel is delivered directly to agricultural customers throughout the service territory. Pricing is structured on regional production costs, not crude oil futures, giving customers a stable, predictable fuel expense.

Service Region

Why East Texas

The East Texas region presents a specific operational opportunity. There is a high concentration of restaurant and food service operations in the mid-size cities, combined with a large agricultural base in the surrounding rural counties.

This geography creates both the supply side and the demand side of the Pines Energy Group model within a manageable operating radius. Restaurants in Lindale, Athens, and the surrounding towns produce waste cooking oil. Farms and ranches in Henderson, Van Zandt, Smith, and neighboring counties need reliable fuel.

Operating within this defined region keeps the company lean, responsive, and directly accountable to the communities it serves.
