Regional Biodiesel Supply for Farms and Ranches

Fuel Supply Operations

Pines BioFuel supplies biodiesel fuel produced from regionally sourced cooking oil collected across East Texas.

The company focuses on long-term fuel relationships with farms, ranches, and agricultural operators seeking reliable fuel supply alternatives.

Operational Advantages

Stable Regional Sourcing

Fuel production built around regional feedstock collection rather than imported crude supply chains.

Cleaner Combustion

Biodiesel supports cleaner engine combustion characteristics compared to conventional diesel fuels.

Reduced Emissions

Biodiesel usage can contribute to lower lifecycle emissions when compared with petroleum diesel.

Operational Consistency

Regional production and local distribution support more predictable supply relationships.
