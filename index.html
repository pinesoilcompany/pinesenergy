How It Works

Two Sides of One Operation

Pines Energy Group connects two regional industries that are rarely linked. Restaurants produce waste oil they need removed. Farms and ranches need reliable, cost-stable diesel fuel. We operate the infrastructure between them.

Supply Side

Pines Oil Recycling

We collect used cooking oil directly from restaurants throughout East Texas. Storage containers are provided on-site at no cost. Our trucks handle pickup on a scheduled or on-call basis.

  • Free oil storage containers provided
  • Scheduled and on-call pickup service
  • No cost to the restaurant
  • Fully licensed waste oil transport
  • East Texas route coverage

Learn About Collection
Demand Side

Pines BioFuel

Collected oil is processed into biodiesel at our Athens, TX facility. The finished fuel is distributed directly to farms and ranches as a clean-burning, price-stable alternative to petroleum diesel.

  • B20 and B100 blend options
  • Compatible with existing diesel equipment
  • Stable regional pricing independent of crude markets
  • Direct delivery to farm or ranch
  • Long-term supply agreements available

Operations

Built for East Texas. Run In-House.

Every aspect of the Pines Energy Group operation is handled directly. We do not broker oil collection to third parties, and we do not purchase feedstock from outside the region when regional supply is available.

This structure keeps costs predictable, quality consistent, and relationships local.

  • 01
    Collection Scheduled oil pickup from restaurant and food service accounts throughout East Texas.
  • 02
    Processing Raw used cooking oil is processed and refined at our Athens, TX facility into biodiesel feedstock.
  • 03
    Production Transesterification converts refined feedstock into finished biodiesel fuel meeting ASTM D6751 standards.
  • 04
    Distribution Finished fuel is delivered directly to farms, ranches, and fleet customers across the service region.
