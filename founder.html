Operational Vision

Regional Energy Infrastructure With a Clear Job to Do

Used Oil Collection & Biodiesel Supply

Brant Borden founded Pines Energy Group LLC to connect two practical needs in East Texas: restaurants need dependable used cooking oil removal, and farms, ranches, and diesel fleets need fuel suppliers that understand regional operations.

The company is organized around direct control of the work. Pines Oil Recycling handles account setup, container placement, and collection routes. Pines BioFuel converts that recovered oil into biodiesel and manages fuel supply relationships with agricultural and fleet customers.

The purpose is simple: keep a regional waste product in productive use and turn it into fuel for the same communities that helped create it.

That local structure is the center of the business. By focusing on a defined East Texas service radius, Pines Energy Group can keep communication direct, routes manageable, quality accountable, and pricing tied to regional feedstock and production realities.
